- `path-present-perfect-for-since` — How long…? + for (a period) / since (a starting point) with present
  perfect: I have lived here for two years / since 2024; contrast with past simple (I lived there for two

- `path-possessive-pronouns` — mine / yours / his / hers / ours / theirs, Whose…?, myself / yourself
