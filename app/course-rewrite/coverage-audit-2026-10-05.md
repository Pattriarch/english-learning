## 5. "~600 lessons" — where the figure comes from

- Path: 171 ids (170 lesson files + missing path-question-tags). Course files: 200 lessons = 170 path + 30 cinema (Better Call Saul, B1–C1, off-path). Plus 19 optional overview units in `curriculum.json` and 24 projects.
- Separate **book library**: `app/content/book-lessons/` = **872 lesson files** (book-release.json: 872/872 published 2026-09-10), from 10 unique "in Use" books in `library.json`: Essential Grammar 115, English Grammar in Use 145, Advanced Grammar 100, Vocabulary Elementary 60 / Upper-int 101 / Advanced 101, Collocations 60+60, Phrasal Verbs 70+60. Opened in the app at `#/books` ("Все учебники"). A further 59 units from 6 new coursebooks are only at intake (`new-coursebooks-intake.json`), not published.
- The ~600 figure most likely comes from the book build in progress: docs of 2026-09-10 mention "576 готовых уроков" (BOOK-QUALITY-SAMPLE) and "617 / 619 готовых книжных уроков" (BOOK-REQUIRED-SOURCE-AUDIT). No doc promises ~600 path lessons.
- Book units were **not merged** into path lessons and **not dropped**: they are a parallel library. `docs/ONE-PROGRESSIVE-COURSE.md`: the daily plan does not run Murphy chapters in parallel with the path. `study-route.json` links only 16 book units to path lessons as "related". The authored course itself shrank from 211 (Sept 10: 185 without cinema = 163 main + 22 optional overviews) to 170 + 5 new ids.
- Most grammar items MISSING above (mine/whose, myself, could/was able to, for/since, so/such, -ed/-ing, it's time, had better, be used to, question tags, the + place names, It is said that) exist as Murphy units in the library — they are not linked from the path.
